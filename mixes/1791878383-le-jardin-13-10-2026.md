# Le Jardin - 13/10/2026 - RnB through the years

## Description

Hi everyone, welcome to another episode recorded live on the [Fréquence Banane](http://web.archive.org/web/20260912165154/https://www.frequencebanane.ch/) student radio. This time I wanted to have half Jazz/RnB from before the 2000s and half Jazz/RnB after the 2000s. It made for an interesting mix of songs. You can join the [group](http://t.me/lejardinfb) and chat with me and others by joining for next time. Thank you very much for listening and have a great week! <3
