# Le Jardin - 05/11/2026 - Mixers

## Description

Hi everyone, welcome to another episode of Le Jardin recorded live on the [Fréquence Banane](http://web.archive.org/web/20260912165154/https://www.frequencebanane.ch/) student radio. Really happy with how this one turned out! Have some songs I love in the beginning and my first time doing something that starts to look like DJing. I've also secretly been making the audio quality better and better, so for those who are able to notice, you'll be happy to know this mix is lossless using FLAC files I've been collecting. As always, thank you for listening and have a great week <3

P.S. You can check out "A CENTURY IN SOUND : The Art of Listening" Kickstarter [here](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/acenturyinsound/a-century-in-sound-the-art-of-listening/)
