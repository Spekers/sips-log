Czech Tearoom Alternative

Date: Tuesday April 7, 2026

Windows Media Player
Description

This mix is based around ambient music from Czechoslovakia and Czechia. After the fall of the Czechoslovak communism regime in 1989, tearooms became the spot for artists to explore new music. Ranging from ambient, new age, folk and minimalism using loops and self-made instruments.

The inspiration came from an NTS mix from Miloš Hroch called “NTS Guide to: Czech Tearoom Ambient”. It threw me down a rabbit hole of impossible to read articles and obscure records.
