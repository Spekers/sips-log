# October Update

Hi Everyone, hope you are doing alright. I hope the colder weather and rain haven’t stopped you from having a good time. I wanted to give a small update on a few things and hope you'll find any of them interesting. 

## Radio

A big thing I've been very invested in recently has been my new morning radio show. It isn't anything groundbreaking and it's heavily borrowing from a much better show hosted by Flo Dill. However, researching songs and having a creative outlet to keep me grounded has been a blessing. The comments and feedback I've had on the last episode were more than I ever expected and I can't thank everyone enough. I'm hoping to keep up my research of different music and hope to set up a live chat sometime where people could tell each other how their morning is going and what they're excited for. I'd love nothing more than to connect random people.

## Free Time

I have fewer courses this semester which gives me more time for hobbies and personal projects. I won't complain because god knows I prefer having my schedule rather than any 30-credit EPFL winter semester, although this change of pace has presented surprising challenges. I'm having a hard time choosing what to invest my free time on. It's easy to take each day as it comes and fill them with fun but I'd like to start some long-term projects. The radio show, sewing clothes, and writing essays are what I've settled on right now and I think that's more than enough. Another "project" is meeting new friends. I have a bunch of people in my life that I'd like to spend more time with but I keep finding excuses not to ask them to grab a coffee. I want to be someone who's reliable, interested in others, and a worthwhile someone's time. I don't want to spread too thin either. There are just too many people I admire and want to get to know. There are, of course, worse problems to have.

## Media
Some articles I'd like to recommend are Danielle Gang's [interview](https://pilotmagazine.uk/article/daniel-arnold-is-emboldened-to-fail-while-holding-new-yorks-hand) of Daniel Arnold. His dedication to his work and his relationship with failure was a nice reminder that for any hobby/art, failure is part of the process. It can even be the best part. Secondly, an old Terry Gross [interview](https://www.npr.org/2025/10/27/nx-s1-5585123/cameron-crowe-memoir-almost-famous-uncool) with Cameron Crowe falls in the same vein. He speaks of his experience hanging around "cool" people and how that shaped him. I think it gives a nice alternative to being around people we aspire to be like. Lastly, I want to recommend [Marco Pierre White's address](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U-xCIstDBaI&t=808s) at Oxford. After only a couple of questions, he starts telling his entire life's story. I think his storytelling and dedication shine through here, and he's captivating to listen to. He has his character flaws and is by no means a perfect person, but I can't help but be inspired.

## Current Book
I'm currently slowly going through Asako Yuzuki's book called "Butter": "The cult Japanese bestseller about a female gourmet cook and serial killer and the journalist intent on cracking her case. Inspired by the real case of the convicted con woman and serial killer, "The Konkatsu Killer," Asako Yuzuki’s Butter is a vivid, unsettling exploration of misogyny, obsession, romance, and the transgressive pleasures of food in Japan." The descriptions of food and the narrator's relationship to what she's eating are very uniquely described. It's been a really interesting read.
